<p>
I received my PhD from the <a href="https://statistics.sciences.ncsu.edu/" target="_blank">Department of Statistics</a> at North Carolina State University. My research interests include causal inference and survival analysis for both observational and randomized studies, the analysis of novel and composite endpoints, data fusion, and federated learning for multi-source biomedical data, sample size calculation and monitoring for clinical trials. I am also broadly interested in applications to cardiovascular outcome trials, HIV/AIDS, congenital heart defects, and racial disparities in healthcare.
</p>
