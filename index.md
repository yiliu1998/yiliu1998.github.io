Thank you for visiting Yi Liu’s website! 

I am a Post-Doctoral Research Fellow at <a href="https://www.fredhutch.org/en.html" target="_blank">Fred Hutchinson Cancer Center</a>, working with Professor <a href="https://www.fredhutch.org/en/people/z/yingqi-zhao.html" target="_blank">Yingqi Zhao</a>. 	

I received my PhD from the <a href="https://statistics.sciences.ncsu.edu/" target="_blank">Department of Statistics</a> at North Carolina State University. My research interests include causal inference and survival analysis for both observational and randomized studies, the analysis of novel and composite endpoints, data fusion for multi-source biomedical data, sample size calculation and monitoring for clinical trials. I am also broadly interested in applications to early cancer detection, cardiovascular outcome trials, HIV/AIDS, racial disparities in healthcare, etc. 

During my PhD study, I was also a Biostatistics Student Fellow at <a href="https://dcri.org/" target="_blank">Duke Clinical Research Institute</a>, under the supervision of Professors <a href="https://dcri.org/personnel/huiman-barnhart-phd" target="_blank">Huiman Barnhart</a>, <a href="https://dcri.org/personnel/roland-matsouaka-phd" target="_blank">Roland Matsouaka</a>, <a href="https://dcri.org/profile/sean-michael-obrien" target="_blank">Sean O'Brien</a>, and <a href="https://dcri.org/personnel/yuliya-lokhnygina-phd" target="_blank">Yuliya Lokhnygina</a>.
